# Exact per-vertex probability certificates without symmetry

This fixed selected pool is pinned to experiment commit
`3536304a360437b6c54413602377c31438c719f0`. All **124 selected representatives**
were processed in **35.56 seconds** of a 60-second budget. Three already failed
the existing fast structural screens. Of the other **121 LP attempts**, **93
produced exact rational scalar certificates** and 28 remained inconclusive.

Eleven of these certificates concern representatives already excluded in the
02:00 audit. The other **82 add new exclusions within this selected pool**, so
the audit and this trial together exclude **101 of the 124 representatives**.
These counts are checked against the immutable audit selection and exclusion
list. They are not a census of all published graphs or possible GHZ supports.
Ninety certificates concern graphs generated by the asymmetric worker; the
other three come from the C2-pairs, C3-mixed and C2-mixed workers. Worker origin
is not a claim about a graph's exact automorphism group.

The new checker `search/event30_vertex_probability_lp.py` applies the trivial
group's thirty singleton orbits, so it needs no symmetry or equal probabilities.
Its relaxation includes:

- the strict bounds **0 < c_v < 1/9**, never the five-orbit lower bound 4/45;
- arbitrary rational three-sector Cauchy inequalities;
- Theorem 56's strict weighted-degree bounds;
- Theorem 53's verified bounds on every eligible disjoint-neighborhood subset;
- the nonstrict bound **c_v <= 1/10** inside a closed degree-two subset, from
  the first half of Corollary 57b, even without a whole context in that subset.

Every saved exclusion has a positive exact contradiction margin. Its proof
uses only sector rows, disjoint-subset bounds and the 1/9 upper bounds. The
weighted-degree and closed-subset rows yielded no used multipliers in this
batch. Unlike a disjoint partition alone, a Farkas combination can use
overlapping subsets and couple their bounds to probabilities in other contexts.

The highest-scoring scalar-certified representative has numerical theta
2.9147908752. The new overall guide at 2.9156526869 remains inconclusive for
this LP, although the separate audit supplies an exact theta dual. Failure to
produce a scalar certificate is not a feasibility or realization result.

The sparsest proofs use twelve inequalities: six sector rows, four disjoint
subset bounds and two 1/9 bounds. For example, graph `3615bc63572950f0aa3d` has
margin **100561/81271584**, with disjoint subset sizes **3,3,5,2**. Its sector
weights are permutations of `(1/2,1/4,1/4)`. More generally these simple weights
account for 894 of the 976 sector rows in the saved proofs.
Removing only the graph field leaves 48 distinct coefficient/descriptor
templates among the 93 certificates. The largest identical templates certify
11, 7, 7, 6 and 6 saved representatives respectively. This observation suggests
possible exact reuse on nearby mutations; no additional graphs were counted
or screened using template reuse in this trial.

Verification is standard-library exact arithmetic and checks graph6 bindings,
moment certificates, original whole neighborhoods, nonnegative multipliers,
coefficient cancellation, strictness and every input/output hash:

```sh
python search/event30_vertex_probability_lp.py
python search/overnight30_probability_reviews/2026-11-05T0200/verify.py
```

Controls include a strict zero-margin weighted-degree contradiction, the
historical leader's two-block moment proof with margin 1/32, a nonstrict closed
subset cap, and eight false-certificate/geometry cases. Independent review
checked the mathematics and repeated the control cases. Optimized Python is
explicitly refused. Older certificate formats and frozen catalogs are unchanged.

This is postprocessing research. No automatic per-candidate LP worker filter
was added, and no event-number or dimension bound changed.
