# Briefing — Tuesday 27 October 2026

Written retrospectively on 30 Oct from the day's commit (`e8bed63`); the question was "does the graph spectrum
say anything about the smallest 3D KS set?".

## Found (ks3d/RESULTS.md §7)
- **Spectra cannot see the 3D KS property** (Theorem 7.1). For any rays in ℂ³, ϑ(Ḡ) = ξ_f(G) = 3, so every weighted
  Hoffman, inertia or p-energy bound on χ(G) is at most 3. Also ϑ(G, w_T) equals the number of bases, while KS needs
  α(G, w_T) to be smaller by one. So the ϑ screen of §6 can never fire, for the graph or for its complement.
- **The first algebraic certificate is at degree 4** (Proposition 7.2, Theorem 7.3). No Nullstellensatz refutation
  of 0-1 colourability of degree ≤ 3 exists. The 31-ray set and Peres's 33 have exact degree-4 refutations over ℚ.
- **Embeddability filters**: on the 17 minimal unembeddable graphs of Li–Bright–Ganesh, unweighted spectra refute
  none, a degree-2 Born SDP refutes 4, and a degree-3 version 8. The other 4 are complex-embeddable.
