# Briefing — Thursday 5 November 2026 (overnight 4–5 Nov)

The question (4 Nov, evening) was to try **route 1** on the remaining dimension-19 case: a design argument for the
three transfer matrices at once, generalising Theorem 36. The suggested starting point was to construct examples
that fall in the remaining class and look for further obstructions. Everything is in RESULTS.md **§2.5**.

## Headline
1. **Not closed: H33's dimension minimality is still open.** Every example we could construct in the remaining
   class fails, and we now know exactly why each one fails. The general non-aligned case is still open.
2. **Transfer structure (Lemma 42).** In dimension 19, Ω = Y_AB⁻¹ Y_AC Y_CB is the event matrix of
   T_B = Q_B E − 1/2 on S_B ⊖ ψ.
   * Every B-neighbourhood is a *singular set* of Ω, and the row of overlaps is its null vector.
   * In the aligned case Ω is normal with spectrum {1, conj τ_j/|⟨α_j|β_j⟩|²}.
   * Theorem 36 is exactly the case of one eigenvalue on p_B^⊥.
   * With two or more eigenvalues the edge overlaps stop being proportional to √(c_a c_b), which is where its
     integrality argument stops working.
3. **When do the projections split into 2×2 blocks? (Proposition 43, new.)**
   * Criterion: **E² commutes with the three projections ⟺ the triple is aligned** (nine 2-dimensional blocks, a
     line of each context in each). The proof: the anticommutators of the reflections 2A_k − 1 are then central, so
     the blocks are Clifford-algebra irreducibles of dimension ≤ 2.
   * **E² scalar** (A + B + C has only the eigenvalues 4/3 and 5/3) forces:
     * all probabilities equal to 1/10;
     * Re τ_j = −1/9 in every block;
     * the weighted cubic identity Y³ = (7/9)Y + (2/9)(J − I), the weighted form of Proposition 37's A³ = 7A − 6I + 6J.

     This case strictly contains Theorem 36's isoclinic triples.
   * An aligned triple has σ = Σ tr(A_kA_l) = 7. With Proposition 37 this shows: **an aligned realization with uniform
     probabilities needs a four-element neighbourhood**.
   * Aligned "design identities": the A-event matrices P = Y_ABY_BA, Q = Y_ACY_CA and M = Y_ABY_BCY_CA commute, and
     PQ(I + M + M† − P − Q) = MM†.
4. **Constructed examples and why they fail (Proposition 44).**
   * A group acting transitively on each context forces uniform probabilities. If the permutation module is
     multiplicity free, d = 19 forces an aligned triple whose blocks are the constituents.
   * **S5 on the 10 two-subsets (Petersen scheme).** This is a genuinely non-isoclinic candidate, with pair cosines
     2/3 four times and 1/3 five times. Disjointness is the only possible support. It forces block overlaps −2/3 and
     1/3, and the two block Gram matrices then have determinants **−25/27 and 20/27** instead of 0.
   * **Z10.** The construction becomes nine "per-character" equations.
     * Support sizes (3,3,3) are impossible analytically: flatness is forced, and then the character χ = 5 fails.
     * For the other support classes with degrees 3–4, least squares finds no solution (smallest residual 0.41).
5. **Symmetric supports already fail without any rank assumption (Theorem 45, exact).**
   * Setting: every maximal triangle-free (10,10,10) support invariant under Z10 or D5 (regular), A5 or S5 (2-subsets),
     F20 or Z2⁴⋊Z5.
   * Result: ϑ(H̄) < 3, with rational dual certificates for all 1279 supports. The largest bound is 2.867, for F20.
   * So no 30-event GHZ realization in any dimension has such a support. Section 1.1 had scanned Z10 and D5
     numerically; the certificates and the non-regular actions are new.
6. **Searches without symmetry are only weak evidence.**
   * The best 30-event support found has ϑ = 2.93747, with exact bound 2.9385 (`search/best30_support.json`). That is
     above the best asymmetric value (2.9123) reported by the parallel campaign on the four-context branch.
   * The same search reaches 2.999988 at 36 events, where GHZ graphs exist. That graph is a near miss, not a GHZ
     graph.
   * It stalls at 2.95 at H33's sizes (9,12,12), where the answer is unique. So it does not find rare graphs.
   * Independent cross-check: my Z5 two-orbit search and that branch's "C5-double" search stop at the same value,
     ϑ = 2.9134341612.
7. **The four-context branch moved overnight** (`research/ghz-gaps-four-context` plus checkpoints on
   `experiments/ghz30-20261104`). It runs a large 30-event campaign and adds new results.
   * I checked its Proposition 39 and Corollary 40 and they are correct.
     * Proposition 39: any two events of a context are joined by a 3-path through the other two contexts, by a
       contraction argument.
     * Corollary 40: an orbit-counting bound on fixed vertices.
   * Its 22:00 review verifies cleanly: 26 exact certificates, the integer-factor bound 2.913630 for the C5 leader,
     and 71 isomorphism claims. `search/event30_orbits.py` also runs as described.
   * Its numbers 39–41 clash with this branch's Lemma 39 to Remark 41 and my new 42–45, so renumber when merging.

## What this changes
* Dimension 19 forces exactly 30 events (Theorem 31). Every construction we could build fails already at the level
  of its support, in every dimension. The cleanest route to d_min = 20 may therefore be to show that **no
  (10,10,10) support has ϑ(H̄) = 3**, which would give n_min ≥ 31 as well. RESULTS.md restates this as a linear
  matrix inequality (LMI) alternative: ϑ(H̄) < 3 exactly when some symmetric X, supported on same-context pairs and
  cross non-edges, is negative definite on {v : equal context sums}.
* Within dimension 19 the open cases are now explicit:
  * aligned with non-uniform probabilities;
  * aligned with uniform probabilities and a four-element neighbourhood;
  * everything non-aligned.

  The E²-scalar case has extra rigidity, though none of it has produced a contradiction yet:
  * saturated events have flat two-step rows;
  * a (4,4) event lives only on "special" blocks, where its context's Bloch vector is antipodal to n̂, with squared
    cosines 1/6, 1/6, 4/9;
  * a (2,4) event lives only on the special blocks of the context holding its four neighbours;
  * each context holds at most one of these kinds, and there are at most nine (4,4) events.

## Caveats
* σ = 7 holds only for aligned triples. On random slack-1 triples σ ranges over 6.985–7.010, so the aligned counting
  holds only approximately in general.
* A support-fixed least-squares solver now finds H33 from random starts every time (calibration passed). It has
  nothing to work on, because no 30-event support with ϑ = 3 is known. A variant that lets the support emerge from
  the overlaps collapses to degenerate supports and was dropped.

## Next steps

   contradiction. This would be the first non-isoclinic exclusion.
2. **Aligned, non-uniform.** Combine the design identities with Lemma 14.
3. **Rank-free route.** Look for vertex-weighted dual certificates for (10,10,10). A uniform polynomial ansatz in
   the adjacency matrix does not work (bound 3.000). Alternatively, attempt an exhaustive Z5 two-orbit enumeration.
   That family contains every family of Theorem 45 and allows two probability values per context.
