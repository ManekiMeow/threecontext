## Found
- **H33 cannot sit inside a K4-free four-context KS set** (Theorem 19, RESULTS.md §3.11). H33's forced realization has
  overlap 1/2 between parallel lines, above the 1/3 cap that Lemma 14 imposes in Route A. So the smallest known GHZ
  graph is of no use as a ray neighbourhood. §3.11 records the failed constructions on H33 in d = 20 and the
  structure behind them.
- **Rigid events force large contexts** (Theorem 21, Corollary 22, §2), for three-context GHZ graphs. An event of
  probability exactly 1/9 is "rigid". Two rigid events in one context force at least 11 events in each other
  context, and three force at least 12, which is sharp in H33. Below 33 events, a context has at most one rigid
  event, even approximately. The spread case is still open. Hand proof, checked by exact LP/MILP
  (`search/rigid_events.py`).
- **A family of GHZ graphs from affine planes and nets** (§1.8). Every net of order q with r parallel classes gives a
  three-context GHZ graph, realizable iff r(q − 2) ≥ 2(q − 1); here the parallel postulate is the paradox. H33 is the
  smallest member.
- **The qutrit picture of H33** (§1.9): its points and lines are qutrit phase-point operators and MUB projectors. It
  is the operator lift of the Hesse configuration. There is no reduction to the 3D KS minimum.
