## The paper (references/)
Yu, Simnacher, Nguyen, Gühne, *Quantum-inspired hierarchy for rank-constrained optimization*, PRX Quantum 3, 010340
(2022). It is filed with notes in `references/README.md`.
- **Useful in principle.** Our realizability question is literally its §III B ("orthonormal representation in
  dimension k"). The hierarchy turns the rank constraint into separability and gives certificates of non-existence,
  which our numerics cannot.
- **Not practical for the candidates.** I implemented level 2 with PPT (`ks/rankhier.py`) and validated it on C₅.
  - On the Shrikhande pattern (16 rays, provably not four bases of ℂ⁴) level 2 is *feasible* at k = 4, even with the
    basis-completeness identities added.
  - So level 2 is weaker than our local ϑ test, which the candidates pass.
  - Level 3 would need (ℂ⁴ᵖ)^{⊗3} with 4p ≥ 388, out of reach even on a cluster.
